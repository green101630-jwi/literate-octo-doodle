Portfolio figure sources — existing evidence, reviewed 2026-11-03

All six charts are drawn from preserved experimental records. No new training,
policy inference, simulator run, or physical robot trial was performed to make
these figures. Full chart values, sample counts, conditions, and source hashes
are in evidence-data.json; accessible descriptions are in chart-manifest.json.

visual-act-tc-e5000.svg / .png
Visual ACT and TC-ACT at epoch 5000. Source: preserved summary_5seeds.json,
ACT-vs-TC evaluation dated 2026-10-23. Five evaluation seeds (2026–2030),
250 paired rollouts each, 1,250 per model and task. Training seed 0.
Evaluation seed 2026 reused an earlier paired evaluation. These are simulator
results from one trained checkpoint per method/task, not five training runs.

spatial-generalization-e6000.svg / .png
Source: two preserved summary_5seeds_e6000.json files covering XY half-ranges
2/4/6 cm and 3/5 cm. Every value was cross-checked against the audit CSV.
Task-specific default colliders; Basket AMP off / Handover AMP on; epoch6000;
training seed 0. Five evaluation
seeds, 250 paired rollouts per seed, 1,250 per model/task/range. Half-range is
not radius. The Basket decomposition / AMP-on series is excluded.

diffusion-steps.svg / .png
Source: preserved fixed-placement rollout JSON for the Diffusion34 no-cube
observation model. The same trained checkpoint was evaluated with 4 denoising
steps, 10 steps, and 10 steps plus a compiled U-Net. Evaluation seed 2026,
250 simulator rollouts per setting, temporal aggregation enabled. The plots
show task success, not measured inference latency or physical success.

diffusion-latency.svg / .png
Source: preserved deployment review of the August 2026 presentation and code.
The presentation reports p99 U-Net inference latency of 35.7 ms eager FP32
and 11.33 ms compiled FP32. A separate 40-second real-joint-feedback shadow
reports 13.37 ms p99 policy request latency, with 50 Hz policy / 100 Hz control.
The shadow involved no physical motion. Benchmark and shadow are displayed in
separate panels because they measure different scopes. Original benchmark
and shadow JSON were not located; these are presentation-reported values.
The 20 ms dashed reference marks a 50 Hz policy period.

real-camera-phase-ablation.svg / .png
Source: September deployment notes, lines 214–218, corroborated by the saved
September presentation review. Visual TC-ACT training seed 0. The values are
user-observed physical success/attempt totals: Basket14D 21/25; Basket17D 0/25;
Handover14D 23/25; Handover17D 20/25. 14D uses joint positions; 17D adds phase.
A complete trial-level ledger and model-hash mapping are unavailable. These
counts are not a reconstructed trial log. Unconfirmed seed-1/2 totals are omitted.

success-rollout-controls.svg / .png
Source: preserved comparison_summary.json, Basket Mimic50 six-control study
dated 2026-10-17. Expert means 50 Mimic demonstrations. Expert-only runs used
14,000 optimizer updates; expert50 plus self-success50 used 14,001. Two
training seeds, 250 simulator rollouts per model at evaluation seed 2026.
The negative result applies to this protocol and does not establish that all
self-generated demonstrations reduce performance.

All success-rate axes run from 0% to 100%; latency axes start at 0 ms.
No confidence intervals or significance claims were inferred. Evaluation-seed
repetition and training-seed repetition are explicitly distinguished.
Figures were generated with Matplotlib from the public evidence-data.json.
SVG provides editable vectors/text; PNG is 2376 × 1440 pixels for sharing.
